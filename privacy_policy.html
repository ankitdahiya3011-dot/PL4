Open Sky Yoga Privacy Policy

Open Sky Yoga respects your privacy and handles personal information in accordance with applicable UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This policy explains what information we may collect, why we use it, and the choices and rights available to you.

Information we may collect Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, contact details, booking and attendance information, payment-related information, and messages you send us. If you choose to share information about your health, injuries, or accessibility needs so that we can support your participation in classes, we may also process that information. Please share only what is necessary for your care and safety.

How we use your information We may use personal information to manage class bookings and memberships, provide yoga classes and related services, respond to enquiries, process payments, communicate service updates, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. We will send marketing communications only where permitted by law. You can opt out of marketing at any time.

Lawful bases We process personal information where necessary to provide a service or take steps you request before entering into an agreement, to meet legal obligations, for our legitimate business interests where these are not overridden by your rights, or with your consent. Where we process health information, we will do so only where an applicable condition under data protection law permits it, such as your explicit consent or another lawful condition.

Sharing and transfers We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business, such as booking, payment, communication, or website providers. They may use information only as instructed and where appropriate safeguards are in place. We may also disclose information when required by law. If information is transferred outside the UK, we will use a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards where required.

How long we keep information We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

Your rights Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correct inaccurate information, request its deletion, restrict or object to certain processing, receive certain information in a portable format, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise a right, contact Open Sky Yoga using the contact details through which you normally communicate with us. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and website use If our website uses cookies or similar technologies, these may be used to make the site work, understand how it is used, or support other stated purposes. Where consent is required, we will ask for it and explain how you can manage your preferences through the available cookie controls or your browser settings.

Security We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children Our services are intended for adults unless a class or activity is specifically described as suitable for children. Where we process a child's personal information, we will do so in accordance with applicable law and take appropriate steps to involve a parent or guardian where required.

Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be made available through our usual communication channels. This policy was last updated on 9 October 2026.
